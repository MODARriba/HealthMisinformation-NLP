TOPIC - Detection Of Health-Related  Misinformation Using NLP

Description

Health-related misinformation can negatively impact public understanding and
decision-making. This project focuses on detecting misleading or false health-related
news articles using NLP techniques.
Students will frame the problem as a text classification task and analyze linguistic
and semantic patterns present in reliable and unreliable health information.

Core Tasks
• Text preprocessing and feature extraction
• Training and evaluation of classification models
• Analysis of misclassified health claims

Possible Datasets
• Health misinformation datasets (English)
• Publicly available fact-checking datasets

Future Scope
• Claim-level health information verification
• Integration with trusted medical knowledge sources
